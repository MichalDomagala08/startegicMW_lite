#Wstęp
Twilight was slowly settling over the dense forests surrounding the small village. Night mist drifted between the trees, and somewhere in the distance the wolves were howling. Against this backdrop, two children wandered through the woods in search of food. Adam and Katherine, a brother and sister from a humble family, struggled with the hunger that had settled over their home after the long winter. Orphaned at a young age, they were being raised by their weary aunt, who loved them as if they were her own children but lacked both the strength and the means to provide them with a life of comfort.
#Adam
Adam glanced at Katherine, wiping the sweat from his brow with a weary sigh after another fruitless search for berries. "We can't go on living like this. Every day is just a fight to survive. Do you know how many times I've thought about leaving this village behind and trying my luck somewhere else?" Every day he saw other boys his age who had far more than he did, although they came from peasant families too. People always told him there was no point in envying them, but how could he not, when his own life seemed to be nothing but hunger and hardship? He felt that if he did nothing, he would end up like his father—worn down by life, bent over the fields, never knowing a better future. 
#Katherine
Katherine looked at her brother with deep concern and a hint of pity. "And where would we go, Adam? People suffer everywhere. Some may have more than we do, but they still live in fear of losing it. I'd rather stay here and help the people around me however I can. Aunt has done everything she could for us. Should we really repay her by turning our backs on her?" Life had never been easy for them, but Katherine never forgot the people who had helped them. Their aunt might not have possessed wealth, but she had a generous heart. She was the one who taught Katherine that kindness should be shared, even when you have very little yourself.
